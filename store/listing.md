QueAura

## Short description (80)

Host a trivia night from your phone. Players join by scanning, no app needed.

## Full description (4000)

Turn any room into a quiz night. QueAura runs the whole game from your Android phone or tablet, and everyone plays on their own phone. No app for players to install, no accounts, no fuss: they scan a code and they're in.

PLAYERS JOIN IN SECONDS
Players scan the QR code with their phone camera, pick a nickname and an emoji, and they're playing. It works in any phone's web browser.

PUT IT ON THE BIG SCREEN
Open the game on any smart TV's browser and the whole room sees the questions, the clock, the answers and the scores, with sound.

WORKS ANYWHERE, EVEN OFFLINE
Games run on your own Wi-Fi, from your phone. No internet? QueAura can create its own Wi-Fi network for players to join. Garden parties, cabins, classrooms and pubs with patchy signal all work.

GAME STYLES
• Multiple choice: tap one of up to four answers before time runs out.
• Closest without going over: everyone types a number; the closest guess that isn't over wins.
• Fastest finger: the first right answer scores the most.
• Questionnaire: everyone answers at their own pace, with no clock and no points. Every answer is saved on your phone, and you can export the results as a spreadsheet file.

YOUR QUESTIONS, YOUR GAMES
• Ready-made starter games so you can play in the first minute.
• Write your own questions, then build games from them with a step-by-step guide that explains every choice.
• Fill a game automatically from your questions, then swap anything you don't like.
• Choose the timing and the scoring: all or nothing, or faster answers score more.

LET AI WRITE THE QUESTIONS (OPTIONAL)
Pick a topic and a difficulty, and AI writes the questions, with answers and explanations. You check every question before it's saved. New accounts get free AI games to try it, and more are available as in-app purchases. Everything else in QueAura works without an account.

YOU'RE IN CHARGE
Pause, skip, or stop a game at any time, remove a player, and play another round with the same players. Questions, game names and nicknames are checked against a list of offensive words, because games show on shared screens.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Games run on your phone and your network. Players give only a nickname, which never leaves your phone. No ads, no tracking.

QueAura is for the person running the night. Hosts need an Android phone or tablet; players can use any phone with a web browser.
