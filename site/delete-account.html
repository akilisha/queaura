Delete your QueAura account

An account exists only if you signed in to use AI-written questions. Playing and hosting games need no account, so there may be nothing to delete.

In the app

Open QueAura → Questions tab → the AI questions card → Delete my account, and confirm twice. It happens straight away.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email info@kerbdrop.com from the address you signed in with, asking us to delete your QueAura account. We'll confirm when it's done, usually within a few days and at most 30.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your sign-in (email address, and the name and picture from Google if you used Google), your balance of AI games and its history, and the records of your AI requests. Unused AI games, bought or free, are lost.
  • Kept: records of purchases and refunds, as bookkeeping law requires. They are kept under a random id, with no name or email, for as long as that law requires (normally up to seven years).
  • Not affected: your questions, games and questionnaire results, which are only on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.
