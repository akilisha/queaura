Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026

QueAura is a trivia game. A host runs it from an Android app; players join from their phone's web browser on the same Wi-Fi. This policy explains what information is involved, where it goes, and your choices. QueAura is operated by [OPERATOR NAME] ("we"). Questions: support@queaura.com.

In short: games run on the host's own phone and Wi-Fi, not on our servers. Players give only a nickname, which never leaves the host's phone. We only receive information when a host signs in to use AI-written questions or buys AI games, and then only what that needs. We don't sell information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics in the game.

Playing a game (players and the TV)

Players join by opening a web page served by the host's phone over the local network. They choose a nickname and an emoji; no name, email or account is asked for. The nickname, the emoji and the answers stay on the host's phone for the game and are not sent to us or anyone else. The page stores one random token in the browser so a player who reloads gets their seat back; it identifies nothing else. The pages use no cookies, analytics, ads or outside services.

The host's phone keeps a technical log of connections (when players join and leave, their nickname and their phone's local network address) to help with connection problems. It stays on the host's phone.

Hosting without an account

The host's questions, games and questionnaire results are stored only on the host's phone. A host can export questionnaire results as a file and share it with apps they choose; we never receive it. A questionnaire collects only what its questions ask, so a host shouldn't ask for information players wouldn't want shared.

Buying AI games

Purchases are made through Google Play, which handles payment; we never see card details. We receive from Google the product bought, the order and purchase identifiers, and whether it was paid or refunded, so we can add or take back AI games. Each purchase carries a scrambled form of the host's account id, so we can match it to the right account; it reveals nothing about the host.

Server logs

Our backend logs each request's route, result and duration, and events such as purchases and sign-in failures, to keep the service working. Logs don't contain questions, topics, passwords or payment details. Our hosting provider may also record technical details such as IP addresses.

How long we keep it

Account information and AI-game history are kept while the account exists, and purchase records as long as the law requires for accounting. Logs are kept for a limited time by our hosting provider. Ask us to delete your account and we delete it, except purchase records we must keep.

Your choices and rights

You can use QueAura without an account. Signed-in hosts can ask us at support@queaura.com for a copy of their information, to correct it, or to delete their account. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may have further rights, including to object or complain to your data protection authority. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising.

Children

Hosting and signing in are for adults. Anyone may play at a host's invitation; players give only a nickname, which stays on the host's phone. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Security

Our backend is reached only over encrypted connections, and its database connection is encrypted too. Games on the local network use plain web pages, which is why nothing personal is asked of players.

Changes

We'll post any changes here with a new effective date, and tell signed-in hosts in the app if a change is significant.
