The quiz night on your phone.

Host a trivia night from your Android phone or tablet. Everyone plays on their own phone by scanning a code: no app for them to install, no accounts. Put the game on the TV for the room to see.

Games run on your own Wi-Fi, from your phone, so it works anywhere, even without internet. Write your own questions, use the ones that come with the app, or let AI write them for you.
